# The Hunt — current project brief

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Reconciled:** 2026-09-06  
**Authority:** Founder clarification of 2026-09-06 plus preserved governing doctrine

## Product

**FOUNDER DECISION:** The Hunt is an entirely digital treasure-hunt experience inside richly explorable virtual worlds. Players search, observe, interpret clues, manipulate supported virtual objects, and submit server-validated digital discoveries. Required physical travel, geolocation hunting, physical-object searches, sensor-driven physical play, and physical-location evidence are outside the active product scope.

The creative center remains visual discovery, deduction, art, recursive or nested spaces, clue fairness, and a trustworthy shared event. The established Midnight Gallery direction remains usable as a fictional digital setting. Any real prize would be awarded for a verified digital claim and requires separate legal, funding, eligibility, integrity, and founder approval; no real-prize launch is authorized now.

## World direction

**FOUNDER DECISION:** The long-term direction includes immersive high-quality 3D virtual worlds. On supported headsets, participants should eventually be able to walk and, where appropriate, fly virtually. Phone access remains part of the intended experience.

This destination does not select a 3D engine, require one renderer for every client, require all worlds to be 3D, or authorize a runtime rewrite. The existing 2D/deep-zoom implementation and 2D-first sequence remain valid near-term assets rather than a permanent prohibition on 3D. Cross-device competitive parity, comfort, accessibility, and security require evidence before shared ranked play.

## Three separate systems

- **Player application/runtime:** the participant and operator experience—world exploration, clues, interaction, claims, integrity, and reporting.
- **World Foundry:** the internal human-led production system for briefs, assets, recipes, attempts, review, rights, cost/time records, and package preparation. It is not a finished autonomous generator or public editor.
- **World Package:** the intended versioned contract between production and playback. Its present implementation is incomplete as a reproducible frozen artifact.

## Standing doctrine

- Finished quality, fairness, accessibility, rights, provenance, security, integrity, portability, and human accountability remain governing constraints.
- One new original world normally supports each major competitive hunt. Generic tools and components may be reused; a solved competitive layout is not automatically reusable for another prize hunt.
- AI may assist and propose. Humans remain accountable for art direction, world structure, clue fairness, rights, accessibility, security, integrity, and publication.
- No cryptocurrency, NFT, external advertising analytics, or autonomous approval path is authorized.
