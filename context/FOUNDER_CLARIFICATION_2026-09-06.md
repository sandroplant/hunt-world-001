# The Hunt — Founder clarification and authorization boundary

**Recorded:** 2026-09-06, America/New_York  
**Source:** Sandro's explicit message in this conversation, following the September project reconstruction.  
**Status:** Record of the founder's expressed decisions. The assistant's implementation proposals remain recommendations. This file has not yet been applied to the Mac or GitHub repository.

## Confirmed founder decisions

1. **The Hunt is an entirely digital game.** References in Master Plan v11 to locating hidden treasure in the physical world were a miscommunication, not an intended product requirement. No required physical travel, geolocation hunt, physical-object search, or sensor-driven physical hunt belongs in the active product scope.
2. **Immersive 3D is the long-term direction.** Participants should eventually explore virtual worlds through supported headsets, including virtual walking/flying, while phone-based access remains part of the vision. This is not a decision that all clients must use one renderer, that all worlds must immediately become 3D, or that a particular headset/engine/provider is selected.
3. **Consolidation and reconciliation are authorized.** Establish one authoritative project home on the founder's Mac, recover relevant project documents, preserve prior work, and align the active documents with confirmed decisions.
4. **Current research and restart recommendations are requested.** Investigate recent technology and less expensive world formats in light of an effectively zero discretionary budget.

## Boundaries and implications

- This request authorizes a documentation/recovery/research exception to the July freeze. It does not, by itself, approve spending, provider calls, public hosting, production deployment, a schema migration, a platform rewrite, or the specific prototype recommended in the companion memo.
- Existing quality, integrity, rights, accessibility, human-approval and replaceability standards remain in force.
- The older 2D-first implementation sequence and the long-term 3D direction can coexist. Whether and when to change the immediate sequence remains a decision informed by a bounded prototype, not by a model announcement alone.
- The proposed three-view cabinet pilot is a **recommended amendment** to the old 8–12-illustration fun-test scope. Do not falsely record it as already approved.
- The physical-world correction does not by itself choose a prize type, payment mechanism, eligibility policy or jurisdiction. No real-prize launch is authorized.
- A digital scene may depict a real place or use licensed photographs as source material without requiring physical-world gameplay. This is a conceptual distinction, not approval to build a scanning/digital-twin pipeline.
